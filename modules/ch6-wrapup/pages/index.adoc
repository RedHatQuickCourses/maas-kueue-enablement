= The Customer Journey

Goal::

Map the 10 conversational scenarios to specific customer maturity stages and equip learners with practical field guides for immediate use.

Your colleague returns from parental leave next week. You need to hand back three accounts in better shape than you found them — and document what you learned so the next person who covers does not start from zero.

This chapter summarizes the full Cost, Risk, and Revenue framework and provides downloadable assets for translating system metrics into executive value.
