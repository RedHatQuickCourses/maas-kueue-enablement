= Securing Funding & Adoption

Partner with customer finance teams to present clear cost, risk, and revenue metrics, and drive organic platform adoption through self-service workflows.

In the final stage of your cover assignment, you have successfully cleared the technical bottlenecks across all three accounts.
But you face one final hurdle: the business sponsors refuse to remove the human-in-the-loop requirement from their automated AI workflows because they do not trust the model outputs.
This single blocker is killing the customer's intended Return on Investment.

This module proves that if field teams cannot guide high-level strategy early, the customer pays for it during downstream technical implementation. You will differentiate Red Hat from generic strategy firms through platform-rooted financial collaboration.
