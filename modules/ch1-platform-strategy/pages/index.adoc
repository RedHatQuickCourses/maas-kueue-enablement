= The Translation Gap & Platform Strategy

Goal::

Frame the AI offering as a complete "AI Factory" lifecycle and demonstrate how platform-rooted strategy prevents catastrophic implementation failures.

It is your first day covering for your colleague on parental leave. Their primary account — a large commercial enterprise — is threatening to walk. Your predecessor was hyper-focused on implementation details and never helped the customer justify the capital expenditure to their C-suite. You need to reframe the entire conversation.

This module introduces the foundational positioning: Red Hat's value is rooted in being the platform maintainer, not a disconnected business consultancy. You will learn to calculate comprehensive value across the full AI lifecycle.
