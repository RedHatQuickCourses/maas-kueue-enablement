= Risk, Data Governance, & Hybrid Agility

Goal::

Configure the MaaS layer to balance public cloud agility with on-premise data sovereignty, and educate customers on data exposure risk without relying on unbacked claims.

Your third account is a highly regulated financial services firm.
Developer teams have been moving fast by sending proprietary market analysis data to cheap, subsidized public cloud token providers.
The CISO has just stepped in to block all production AI deployment due to massive compliance and data exposure fears.
You need to build a bridge between the conflicting developer and security teams.

This module equips you to defend against vendor lock-in by proving to CISO and Legal that the architecture dynamically balances cloud agility with internal data sovereignty.
