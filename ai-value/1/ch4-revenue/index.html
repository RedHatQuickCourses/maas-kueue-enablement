Revenue & Rescuing "Decision Past"

Goal

Diagnose interconnect saturation in a failing multi-node environment and advise customers on the objective truth about their hardware and model choices.

You are paged into a critical, high-stakes customer escalation that your colleague was managing before their leave. An external strategy partner convinced the customer’s leadership they could rapidly accelerate revenue by launching a massive Mixture of Experts model on a limited 2-node cluster. The environment has suffered a catastrophic performance failure, and the relationship is on the line. You must deliver the objective truth.

This is the core scenario module of the course. You will navigate the CEO’s demand for the "biggest, best model" by reconciling what they want with what their environment provides.
